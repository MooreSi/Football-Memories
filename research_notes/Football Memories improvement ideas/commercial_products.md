# Commercial youth/grassroots sports video and "memories" products: feature inventory (researched 2026-11-16)

Scope: features these products offer that Football Memories does NOT already have (it already has Hudl/Veo libraries, full-match playback with markers, next/prev moment, highlight reel builder with title/score/stats cards, player hub totals/records/streaks, milestones, season story, top 5, head-to-head, momentum chart, PNG/PDF reports, drawing on video, crests, Plex export). Most sources are vendor marketing or help pages; treat accuracy claims as vendor claims. Dates are the publication or launch date where the source gave one; "undated" means the page gave none.

## 1. What do the main vendors offer for individual player highlights, auto-clipping and parent-facing views?

### Takeaway
The common pattern across Veo, Trace, Hudl and XbotGo is a **per-player automatic playlist** (every clip involving one child, built without manual tagging), plus a **visual "follow this player" overlay** (circle, name label, zoom) and a **shareable player profile/showreel**. Newer 2025-2026 launches add AI-written match summaries and multi-story auto-highlights (game recap, per-team story, MVP story, halftime highlights).

### Cited Findings
**Veo**
- Player Spotlight automatically detects shirt numbers to identify players and builds a playlist of each player's key moments; coaches assign shirt numbers in the members list/lineup and players can claim their own jersey in the app; available for outdoor football only; later folded into the Analytics 2 package (search-result summary of Veo pages, undated) — [Veo Player Spotlight](https://veo.co/product/veo-player-spotlight); [Veo announcement](https://updates.veo.co/announcements/ann_93FN3qsJTPLfd)
- "Player Moments" auto-builds individual highlight reels; users can tune sensitivity by the player's distance to the ball, time in possession, and the pre/post-roll length of each clip (undated) — [What is Veo Player Spotlight](https://shop.veo.co/pages/what-is-veo-player-spotlight)
- "Circle the Player" (spring 2025) visually rings a chosen player during key actions; requires Player Spotlight — [Veo article: Circle the Player Q&A](https://www.veo.co/article/circle-the-player-q-a-with-linda-skovmand-and-signe-bering)
- Player Profile: invited players build and share a showreel of their best Veo moments, reachable from Veo Clubhouse or a link; shared highlights autoplay (2025, exact date not confirmed) — [Veo updates](https://updates.veo.co/); [Veo Player Profile](https://www.veo.com/product/player-profile)
- Share With Opponent: share a recording by letting the other side scan a QR code in the camera app (Veo Camera iOS 3.2.0, date not shown); League Exchange share links (9 Jul 2025); iOS 3.5.4 added editable bookmark timestamps (mid-2025) — [Veo updates](https://updates.veo.co/)

**Trace (PlayerFocus)**
- AI follows one child through the game and returns that child's reel; parents subscribe from about $15/month (launch coverage 2023); sharing requires a subscription and downloads require PlayerFocus Pro — [The Fast Mode](https://thefastmode.com/technology-solutions/31850-trace-introduces-playerfocus-ai-based-video-highlight-platform-for-youth-soccer-players); [Core77](https://www.core77.com/posts/136929/Good-or-Bad-This-System-Records-Entire-Sporting-Matches-But-Highlights-Just-Your-Child)
- Two highlight types per player: **Spotlight highlights** (player is the main focus, marked with an icon) and **Tactical highlights** (moments that matter to the player even if off-frame, e.g. a team goal) (help page, undated) — [Trace support](https://support.traceup.com/content/playerfocus-highlights-and-always-on-spotlight)
- **Always-on Spotlight** overlay options: a circle that follows the player, the player's **name label** following them, and an experimental "Hagrid Mode" that **magnifies** the player (undated) — [Trace support](https://support.traceup.com/content/playerfocus-highlights-and-always-on-spotlight)
- Cal South named Trace its exclusive video partner (multi-year); clubs report parents receive personalised reels after games — [Cal South](https://calsouth.com/cal-south-announces-trace-as-new-exclusive-video-partner/)

**Hudl (Assist, Focus, Balltime)**
- Hudl soccer update (4 Aug 2025): **AI Insights** beta for Assist+ teams, AI-written summaries of team performance, attacking/defensive breakdowns and set pieces, each linked to video; **Automatic Athlete Playlists** deliver each athlete's clips as they are recorded; Assist+ now logs unsuccessful passes, crosses and shots; Focus Flex adds auto calibration and a beta remote pan/zoom angle — [Hudl: What's new in soccer](https://www.hudl.com/blog/whats-new-soccer)
- Hudl highlight export offers a **Vertical (9:16)** aspect ratio for social sharing; Balltime has **AI-Generated Highlight Templates** that filter/organise clips by skill or position (undated help pages) — [Hudl Balltime highlights guide](https://www.hudl.com/support/balltime/guides/highlights)
- Assist-based player highlights: filter plays, edit clips, add **spot shadows** (player spotlight) and **music**, share (undated) — [Hudl support](https://www.hudl.com/support/diamond-sports-playbook/guides/how-to-use-hudl-assist-to-create-player-highlights)
- Hudl auto "15-second game reel" of a team's best plays, dependent on jersey numbers in breakdown data (undated) — [Hudl blog](https://hudl.com/blog/you-dominate-the-gridiron-well-handle-the-highlights)

**GameChanger (US; baseball/softball/basketball, not football)**
- Auto-generated newspaper-style **"Game Recap Stories"** written from scoring data, shown in a Recap tab of the box score — [help.gc.com](https://help.gc.com/hc/en-us/articles/360045621491)
- Computer vision auto-captures highlights and produces **radio-style commentary** (Business Insider, Jul 2024) — [Business Insider India](https://businessinsider.in/artificial-intelligence/news/gamechangers-ai-tech-is-helping-support-youth-sports-and-showcase-more-than-7-million-games-annually/amp_articleshow/111785481.cms)
- Video highlights, archives, **career stats** and spray charts are paid features for families (App Store listing, undated) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/CA/app/id1308415878)
- The 2026 GameChanger help article ("GameChanger in 2026") returned HTTP 403; contents not verified — [help.gc.com](https://help.gc.com/hc/en-us/articles/41988821287309-GameChanger-in-2026-Live-the-Game-Like-Never-Before)

**Spiideo**
- AI Highlights add-on to Spiideo Play (available 5 May 2026; soccer, ice hockey, basketball, handball) auto-generates from one game a **Game Recap, Home Team Story, Away Team Story, MVP Story and Halftime Highlights**; uses video, event data, audio commentary and context; multi-format output for broadcast and social; editorial review tools — [Sports Video Group, 7 May 2026](https://www.sportsvideo.org/2026/05/07/spiideo-launches-ai-highlights-for-automated-highlight-and-clip-creation/)

**XbotGo / Pixellot**
- XbotGo Chameleon: pick a jersey number in the app and the AI follows that player for personal highlight reels; upload to cloud for automatic highlights; marketed to parents (vendor page, undated); a KSL parent report notes it can misread jersey numbers, tag the wrong player or miss moments — [XbotGo basketball page](https://xbotgo.com/pages/basketball-auto-tracking-camera); [KSL](https://www.ksl.com/article/51401562)
- XbotGo Falcon, $599 hands-free camera (PetaPixel, 7 Jan 2026); Veo's own comparison rates it as "basic highlights only" — [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2026/01/07/the-hands-free-599-xbotgo-falcon-captures-sports-like-a-tv-broadcaster); [Veo comparison](https://www.veo.com/article/sports-video-camera-systems-compared)
- Pixellot Air NXT portable AI camera with a tournament mode for back-to-back games — [Broadcast Bridge](https://thebroadcastbridge.com/content/entry/20840/introducing-pixellot-air-nxt-the-next-generation-multi-sports-portable-ai-c)

### Inferences
- Football Memories already has the tagged-moment data that these vendors derive by AI. The cheap wins are presentational: a **"Spotlight vs Tactical" split** of the child's moments (moments he did vs team moments he was on the pitch for), a **follow-the-player overlay** (circle/name label/zoom) done by manual keyframes on top of the existing drawing-on-video feature, and **per-clip pre/post-roll controls** like Veo's sensitivity sliders.
- Spiideo's "story" templates (Game Recap, our-team story, MVP story, halftime highlights) map neatly onto auto-generated reels from existing tags: one-click "first-half reel", "his match reel", "team goals reel".
- Hudl/GameChanger AI summaries suggest a **written match recap** generated from local event data (score, scorers, momentum, his involvement), produced offline from templates rather than an external LLM, to keep the archive local.

### Gaps
- Could not verify Veo Analytics/Veo Live 2025-2026 specifics (e.g. AI commentary) because searches for "Veo" were swamped by Google's unrelated Veo AI model.
- No usable sources found for Playsight, Once Sport, BallerTV, Playon, Ballogy, SportsRecruits or Hudl IQ within the time budget.
- GameChanger's 2026 release article was blocked (403).

## 2. Which products do "player-follow" or auto-highlight of one player, and how?

### Takeaway
Shirt-number recognition is the dominant method (Veo Player Spotlight, XbotGo, Hudl via tagged jersey numbers). Trace's identification method is not stated on its help page. None of the sources found used wearables or GPS for highlight selection.

### Cited Findings
- Veo: automatic **shirt-number detection**; numbers assigned by coach in lineup or claimed by the player — [Veo Player Spotlight](https://veo.co/product/veo-player-spotlight)
- XbotGo: user **selects a jersey number** and the camera physically follows that player; another XbotGo page describes tracking by **jersey colour**, an unresolved inconsistency — [XbotGo](https://xbotgo.com/pages/basketball-auto-tracking-camera); [XbotGo how-to](https://xbotgo.com/blogs/how-tos/jersey-number-tracking-for-basketball)
- Hudl: player reels depend on **jersey numbers entered in breakdown (Assist) data** — [Hudl blog](https://hudl.com/blog/you-dominate-the-gridiron-well-handle-the-highlights)
- Trace: "automatically identifying your player"; method not described on the page — [Trace support](https://support.traceup.com/content/playerfocus-highlights-and-always-on-spotlight)
- Accuracy caveat: jersey reading can misidentify or miss plays — [KSL](https://www.ksl.com/article/51401562)

### Inferences
- For a single-child archive, the practical path is **"claim my number per match"** (shirt number can change season to season) plus manual tap-to-tag, with Veo/Hudl jersey data imported where it exists. No facial recognition is needed or recommended.

### Gaps
- No sources found on wearable/GPS-driven highlights (e.g. Playermaker, Catapult One) in this pass. Facial recognition used by any vendor: none found in sources; not recommended regardless.

## 3. What do consumer memory products do that maps onto a child's football seasons?

### Takeaway
Yearly recaps (Google Photos Recap, Strava Year in Sport, Spotify Wrapped style) combine a short highlight video with a few headline stats, are time-limited events (December), and are built to be shared or edited. Google lets users hide people/photos and regenerate.

### Cited Findings
- Google Photos 2025 Recap (Dec 2025): short highlight video of the year in the Memories carousel; stats like top people and total photos, new selfie count; Gemini-powered hobbies/standout moments (US); **hide specific people or photos and regenerate**; "Edit with CapCut" templates; share direct to WhatsApp Status; pinned in Collections for all of December — [9to5Google, 3 Dec 2025](https://9to5google.com/2025/12/03/google-photos-2025-recap/); [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-google-photos-recap/); [Chrome Unboxed](https://chromeunboxed.com/your-google-photos-2025-recap-is-rolling-out-now-and-this-year-you-can-actually-edit-it/)
- Strava Year in Sport 2025: in-app only, available 8 Dec 2025 to 23 Jan 2026, needs at least three activities, moved behind subscription (drew complaints) — [Strava support](https://support.strava.com/hc/en-us/articles/22067973274509-Your-Year-in-Sport); [Ars Technica via TagTeam](https://tagteam.harvard.edu/hub_feeds/3415/feed_items/17132945/content)
- Apple Photos Memories is credited with good music integration via Apple Music but clunky sharing outside Apple (single comparative remark, not primary) — search snippet only; treat as weak

### Inferences
- Mappable ideas: a **season-end "Wrapped" story** (swipeable cards: goals, minutes, best moment, favourite opponent, first-ever X) released at season end; **"On this day"** surfacing a match/moment from the same date in earlier seasons; **hide-and-regenerate** controls on auto reels; **auto-titled season/tournament albums** from fixture metadata; a December calendar-year recap alongside the football-season one.

### Gaps
- Could not retrieve primary sources for Apple Photos Memories/Recaps or Google Photos "on this day" behaviour within budget; claims about those are not sourced here.

## 4. Parent features: sharing with family, private links, printed books, stat cards, vertical clips, music, captions

### Takeaway
Vendors share via links, QR codes and autoplaying profile pages, offer 9:16 vertical export and music on reels, and gate sharing/downloads behind subscription tiers. No vendor source found offered printed yearbooks/photo books.

### Cited Findings
- 9:16 vertical highlight export for social (Hudl/Balltime) — [Hudl](https://www.hudl.com/support/balltime/guides/highlights)
- Music and spot-shadow effects on player reels (Hudl Assist) — [Hudl support](https://www.hudl.com/support/diamond-sports-playbook/guides/how-to-use-hudl-assist-to-create-player-highlights)
- Link-shared player showreel with autoplay (Veo Player Profile); QR-code sharing of recordings (Veo Camera) — [Veo updates](https://updates.veo.co/)
- Trace: share requires subscription; download requires Pro tier — [Core77](https://www.core77.com/posts/136929/Good-or-Bad-This-System-Records-Entire-Sporting-Matches-But-Highlights-Just-Your-Child)
- Google Photos: one-tap share to WhatsApp Status, CapCut templates — [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2025/12/03/google-photos-2025-recap/)
- Name label that follows the player (Trace) doubles as an on-video caption — [Trace support](https://support.traceup.com/content/playerfocus-highlights-and-always-on-spotlight)

### Inferences
- For a local-first app: **9:16 reframe export** (manual or tag-guided crop window) for WhatsApp/Instagram; **QR code / time-limited private link** served from the home network or exported as a self-contained file for grandparents; **music bed** on reels (from local files to avoid licensing issues); **burnt-in captions** (event, minute, score) reused from existing title/stats cards; a **printable season book** PDF built from existing report PNGs would be a differentiator, as vendors do not appear to offer one.

### Gaps
- No sources found for vendor printed yearbooks, family-specific viewer roles, or auto-captions/subtitles.

## 5. Notable 2025-2026 launches

### Takeaway
AI-written match summaries (Hudl AI Insights, Aug 2025), automatic per-athlete playlists (Hudl, Aug 2025), Circle the Player (Veo, spring 2025), and multi-story AI highlights using commentary audio (Spiideo, May 2026). There is also a rising authenticity concern about AI-generated/altered highlight tapes (2026).

### Cited Findings
- Hudl AI Insights and Automatic Athlete Playlists (4 Aug 2025) — [Hudl](https://www.hudl.com/blog/whats-new-soccer)
- Veo Circle the Player (spring 2025) — [Veo](https://www.veo.co/article/circle-the-player-q-a-with-linda-skovmand-and-signe-bering)
- Spiideo AI Highlights (5 May 2026) — [Sports Video Group](https://www.sportsvideo.org/2026/05/07/spiideo-launches-ai-highlights-for-automated-highlight-and-clip-creation/)
- XbotGo Falcon review (7 Jan 2026) — [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2026/01/07/the-hands-free-599-xbotgo-falcon-captures-sports-like-a-tv-broadcaster)
- AI-generated highlight tapes flagged as a recruiting integrity risk (Feb 2026) — [Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/high-school/football/the-rise-of-ai-generated-highlight-tapes-in-high-school-football-creates-a-dangerous-recruiting-shortcut-01kjd7qa2fbt); [FootballScoop, 27 Feb 2026](https://footballscoop.com/2026/02/27/ai-artificial-intelligence-has-officially-spilled-over-into-highlight-videos-beware)

### Inferences
- The authenticity concern supports keeping reels **provably unedited source footage** (e.g. embed source match + timestamp on each clip card), a natural fit for an archive.
